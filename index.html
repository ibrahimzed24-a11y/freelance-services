sample mini-audit

3 things I'd fix

  1. The photos are too heavy.The home page downloads about 22 MB, and ten of the photos are over 2 MB each. A guest on a phone waits.
  2. On a slow phone, the main picture took about 40 seconds.That was in Google's speed test set to a phone. Many guests would leave before the page opened.
  3. Google gets no clear signal that this is a restaurant.I found no restaurant details for Google or AI answers to read: opening hours, menu link, address.

What already works: a phone number to tap, a map link, and the page fits a phone screen.

a chef's checklist

5 things every restaurant site needs

  • A menu you can readReal words with prices, not a photo you have to zoom into.
  • A booking button that worksOne tap to call, message or book. No hunting.
  • Map and opening hours up frontA guest outside your door should see them in seconds.
  • Opens fast on a phoneMost guests choose where to eat from their phone.
  • Real photos of your food and roomYour own plates, light enough to open quickly.

Sample checked on November 25, 2026 with Google's speed test, set to a phone. It is a lab test, so a real phone will vary. I'm not naming the business.
