Questions

Good to know.

I'm a complete beginner. Is that okay?

Yes. Start with Beginner Yoga or Sunrise Flow. Every move has an easier option.

What should I bring?

Comfy clothes and water. Mats and props are in the studio.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes, up to two hours before class, so someone else can take your spot.

Do you teach one to one?

Yes. Send a message and we will find a time that works.
