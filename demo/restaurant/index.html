Our story

A small kitchen with a big grill.

Ember & Olive is built around one idea: simple food, cooked well. Fish and lamb go on the coals, vegetables come in from nearby growers, and the menu changes with what is good that week.

Come for a long lunch, a shared table of mezze, or a quiet dinner by the window.
