About the studio

Quiet photography for loud, happy days.

Every session starts with a short chat about what matters to you, then the camera mostly gets out of the way.

Questions

Good to know.

How far ahead should I book?

As soon as you have a date. Popular weekends fill first, but short notice is always worth asking about.

Do you travel?

Yes. Travel costs are agreed with you before booking, so there are no surprises.

When will I get my photos?

Portrait and family galleries are ready in about two weeks. Weddings take a little longer.

Can I choose the location?

Of course. A short chat about the place helps plan the best light for it.
