Questions

Good to know.

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 3 pm and check-out is by 11 am. Early or late is easy to ask for.

Is breakfast included?

Yes. A simple breakfast of fresh bread, eggs, fruit and coffee is served on the terrace every morning.

Can I cancel?

Free cancellation up to 3 days before arrival on every room.

Do you take pets or children?

Children are very welcome, and the Garden Twin has a sofa for a third guest. Small, well-behaved dogs are welcome on request.
